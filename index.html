About Data With Sri

Technology learning designed around you.

Data With Sri is an online technology training initiative focused on helping learners build strong technical foundations and practical skills.

We believe learning should be customizable. Tell us what you want to learn and we can customize the training according to your goals, current skill level and requirements.
